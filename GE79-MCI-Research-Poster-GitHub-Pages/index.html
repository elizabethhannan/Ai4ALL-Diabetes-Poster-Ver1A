Type 2 Diabetes & Cognitive Decline

Subtle cognitive decline can begin years before symptoms become obvious. Earlier identification may create a wider window for monitoring and intervention.

POTENTIAL IMPACT & FUTURE WORK

What comes next matters more than one promising score.

POTENTIAL IMPACT

Earlier identification may improve monitoring and support.

  • False positives can trigger unnecessary testing.
  • False negatives risk delayed care.
  • Equity must be evaluated across populations.
FUTURE WORK

Validate before any real-world application.

  • Evaluate GE-75 for future validation.
  • Use larger, diverse, multi-site cohorts.
  • Pair SHAP with longitudinal and causal analysis.
Additional validation using larger, more diverse, multi-center populations is essential before explainable machine learning could support reliable identification of cognitive decline.
